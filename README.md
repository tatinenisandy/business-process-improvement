# Business Process Improvement & ERP Transformation

**Portfolio case study \| Illustrative specialty-retail scenario \|
Synthetic data only**

> This is a portfolio demonstration, not a claim that every artefact was
> used on a specific client engagement. Only present responsibilities
> and outcomes as personal experience when independently verifiable.

## Executive summary

This case study demonstrates a structured approach to improving
disconnected finance, inventory and sales processes during an ERP
transition. The illustrative organisation faces duplicated data entry,
inconsistent product records, spreadsheet-based reconciliations and
limited end-to-end visibility.

## Objectives

1.  Map current processes, pain points and control gaps.
2.  Define prioritised business requirements and process ownership.
3.  Improve the flow of sales, inventory and finance information.
4.  Prepare and reconcile data for migration.
5.  Validate critical workflows through user acceptance testing (UAT).
6.  Support training, operational readiness and BAU transition.

## Scope

**In scope:** order-to-cash, procure-to-pay, inventory, finance
interfaces, master data, reporting, training, UAT and transition
planning.

**Out of scope:** e-commerce platform replacement, tax-policy changes,
unapproved custom development and unrelated HR processes.

## Governance

Sponsor owns business outcomes and escalations; project lead coordinates
scope, schedule, risks and reporting; process owners approve
requirements and designs; IT/vendor teams configure and migrate; super
users validate workflows and support adoption.

## Success measures

Agree baselines and targets before delivery. Measures may include UAT
pass rate, data reconciliation completion, reduction in duplicate entry,
training completion, open defects at go-live and operational exceptions
during stabilisation. Do not represent illustrative targets as achieved
results.

## Key risks

  -----------------------------------------------------------------------
  Risk                                Response
  ----------------------------------- -----------------------------------
  Poor source data                    Profile, cleanse, reconcile and
                                      obtain owner approval

  Scope expansion                     Change control and impact
                                      assessment

  Low adoption                        Early user involvement, role-based
                                      training, super users

  Integration failure                 End-to-end testing and fallback
                                      procedures

  Incomplete UAT                      Trace coverage to requirements;
                                      process-owner sign-off

  Cutover disruption                  Rehearsal, go/no-go criteria and
                                      rollback approach
  -----------------------------------------------------------------------
